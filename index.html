My Story

I thought I knew
everything…

As a biomedical scientist and wellness influencer, I believed I had it all figured out — until I faced multiple health crises, two miscarriages, and unexplainable inflammation in my shoulder and jaw. I spent almost a year in doctor's offices, clinics, and emergency rooms.

This forced me to rethink everything I knew about health, relearn from scratch, and build a new approach from the ground up. What I discovered changed everything about how I understand the body — and recovery.

I developed a Stacking Framework — targeting inflammation, hormones, and cellular repair simultaneously through small daily changes that compound into full-body optimization. This is not one fix. It is a system.

How it Works

The Bounce Back Program is a three-pillar system and multi-factorial intervention to optimize your body. Each pillar addresses a different dimension: your environment, your body, your mind. These pillars are not sequential — you work on all three simultaneously. The improvement on each pillar compounds, giving results that are much faster than working on each pillar alone.
